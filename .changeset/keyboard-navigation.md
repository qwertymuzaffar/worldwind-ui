Keyboard navigation on the globe, on by default: the canvas is focusable and announced as an application; arrow keys pan relative to the heading, `+` and `-` zoom, Shift with the arrows rotates and tilts, PageUp/PageDown tilt, Home resets the orientation. Configure or disable it with the `keyboard` globe option; `handleNavigationKey` is exported for custom bindings.
